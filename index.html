Medicare & Health Coverage

We offer guidance across the full range of Medicare and supplemental health coverage options.

Medicare

Original Medicare

Parts A & B form the foundation of Medicare coverage — hospital care, doctor visits, and outpatient services. We help you understand exactly what’s covered and where the gaps are.

Learn More →
Supplement

Medicare Supplement

Also called Medigap, these plans cover copayments, coinsurance, and deductibles that Original Medicare leaves behind. We compare plans across carriers to find your best value.

Learn More →
Advantage

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one HMO and PPO plans from private insurers that often bundle dental, vision, and drug coverage. We help you weigh network flexibility against added benefits.

Learn More →
Part D

Prescription Drug Plans

Medicare Part D helps cover prescription medication costs. We review your specific drug list to identify the plan with the lowest overall out-of-pocket cost for you personally.

Learn More →
Extras

Dental, Vision & Hearing

Original Medicare doesn’t cover most routine dental, vision, or hearing care. We connect you with affordable supplemental plans that fill these important gaps.

Learn More →
Critical Illness

Cancer / Heart Attack / Stroke

A critical illness diagnosis brings costs Medicare may not cover. These specialized plans pay a direct benefit to you upon diagnosis — for treatment, recovery, or any need.

Learn More →
Indemnity

Hospital Indemnity

Receive a cash benefit for each day spent in the hospital, regardless of other coverage. Ideal for covering Medicare Part A deductibles and daily coinsurance costs that add up quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from people approaching 65 or reviewing their Medicare options.

When should I enroll in Medicare?

Your Initial Enrollment Period (IEP) starts three months before the month you turn 65 and ends three months after — a 7-month window in total. Missing this window without a qualifying Special Enrollment Period may result in permanent late-enrollment penalties on your premiums. We recommend reaching out 6 months before your birthday so we can help you plan ahead.

Does it cost me anything to work with CG Healthcare Group?

No — our services are completely free to you as a client. We are licensed independent agents and are compensated directly by the insurance carriers whose plans we help you enroll in. You receive the same plan at the same premium whether you come through us or go directly to the carrier. There is no catch.

Can I change my plan if I’m not happy with it?

Yes. Medicare plans can generally be changed once per year during the Annual Enrollment Period (AEP), which runs October 15 through December 7, with changes effective January 1. Certain life events — such as moving, losing employer coverage, or gaining/losing Medicaid — may qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period outside of AEP. We contact all of our clients before Open Enrollment each year to review their options together.
